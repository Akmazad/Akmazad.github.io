Who we look for

PhD & MSc researchers

Projects in Bayesian network inference, cancer signalling and drug resistance, network medicine and drug repositioning, single-cell and multi-omics analysis, and trustworthy medical AI. Co-supervision with partner universities is possible.

Undergraduate researchers

IMSIU students can join hands-on projects in Python and R for data science, machine learning and deep learning, often leading to co-authored papers.

Visitors & collaborators

Clinicians, biologists and public-sector partners with data and a question, and methodologists who want to test ideas on real problems. We co-develop grant proposals with RDIA, SNIH and others.

How to apply

  1. Read a few of our papers or method posts and identify a theme that excites you.
  2. Email {{ site.lab.email }} with the subject line "Prospective student – ADNS Lab" and include:
    • a CV with your transcripts or grades,
    • a half-page research statement linking your interests to one of our themes,
    • links to code (GitHub) or writing samples, if any.
  3. Funding: PhD and MSc candidates normally apply through IMSIU's graduate admissions or a partner university's scholarship round; we are happy to advise on proposals.

Helpful background: programming in R or Python, probability and statistics, linear algebra; biology knowledge is a plus but not required.
