Flagship directions

Onco-CDR

A multi-omics and network-medicine platform for next-generation cancer combination therapy, building on DrugSimDB and COVID-CDR.

AI for disability

Two King Salman Center for Disability Research grants supporting HAR, autism, Alzheimer's and lung-disease models, plus multilingual 3D Braille generation.

Public-sector analytics

Crowd-hotspot prediction at Jamarat, food-safety certification with video AI, and topological quality assurance for national surveys.
